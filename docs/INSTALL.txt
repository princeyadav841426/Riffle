RIFFLE — HOW TO INSTALL
=======================

1. Drag Riffle onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Riffle from your Applications folder.

   Riffle is free and isn't sold through Apple, so the first time macOS
   says it can't verify Riffle. That's expected. To allow it:

   macOS 15 Sequoia and later
     • Click Done.
     • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
     • Scroll down to "Riffle was blocked…" and click Open Anyway.
     • Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open Anyway again.

   macOS 14 Sonoma
     • Right-click Riffle in Applications, choose Open, then click Open.

   Or, on any version, paste this into Terminal:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Riffle.app

3. Riffle shows a green tick when it's ready. Close it.

4. Select any folder in Finder and press Space.

Still seeing a plain folder icon? Open Riffle again, click
"Extension Settings…", and make sure Riffle is switched on under Quick Look.

Help and source code: https://github.com/princeyadav841426/Riffle
To uninstall: drag Riffle from Applications to the Trash.
